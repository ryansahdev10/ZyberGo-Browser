## 🚀 What's New in Version 2.6.00

### 🛡️ K15 Roll Out & Privacy Overhaul
*ZyberGo v2.6.00 officially marks the launch of the **K15 Roll Out Addition**, a 4-version consecutive release strategy. Under **K15 Rules**, all browser development targets and feature engineering are officially directed toward maximum privacy, zero-tracking, and network-level anonymity until the next 4 updates.*

- 🧅 **Tor Network Integration:** Built-in native Tor routing support! Route your web traffic through encrypted relay nodes for complete onion-routing anonymity and true IP obfuscation.
- 🕵️ **Fake Identity Mode (User-Agent & OS Spoofing):** Instantly mask your real device fingerprint. Force ZyberGo to report its identity as Safari, Chrome, Edge, or custom OS signatures (macOS, Windows, iOS, Linux) to bypass device tracking and restrictive paywalls.
- 🔒 **Strict Fingerprinting Defenses:** Hardened anti-fingerprinting shields designed to completely neutralize canvas fingerprinting, WebGL tracking, WebRTC leaks, and audio context profiling.
